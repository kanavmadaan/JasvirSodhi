JASVIR’S JOURNEY

A life shaped by service — and a commitment that continues.

I am a mother of two, a proud Ward 2 resident of seven years, a hobby farmer, an entrepreneur and a local mental-health care-home operator. My family and I call this community home, and I want to keep serving the people who make Halton Hills special.

38years of community service
30years serving in Canada
8years serving in the UK

MY ROOTS

Service started early.

I have been serving others since I was 14, carrying forward the example set by my father. He worked as an electrical engineer for 49 years and, during that time, created employment opportunities for about 450 people from families in need.

He taught me that real service is not about recognition — it is about lifting people up, creating opportunity and being there when someone needs help. That lesson has stayed with me throughout my life and continues to shape how I serve today.

WHAT I LEARNED
“Real service means lifting others up.”

That principle has guided my work with families, youth, seniors, newcomers and community organizations across Canada and the UK.

WHAT LEADERSHIP MEANS TO ME

“Leadership is about being a people’s voice and being available to everyday residents whenever they need help.”

I believe good representation means listening, following up and staying accessible — not simply appearing for ceremonies. Residents should know their councillor is willing to hear them and work with them.

MY VISION

A stronger, more self-reliant Halton Hills.

I want to work with residents and every level of government to make Halton Hills a model community — one that protects its rural identity while creating opportunity and maintaining reliable local services.

As an entrepreneur, business owner and hobby farmer, I understand the importance of supporting local business, protecting agriculture and rural life, creating jobs, improving infrastructure and respecting every tax dollar.

What I will keep advocating for

  • Protect hamlets, farms and rural character
  • Safer streets, traffic calming and neighbourhood watch
  • Better roads, drainage and infrastructure
  • A fully equipped and supported fire department
  • Affordable options for seniors to age locally
  • Youth recreation, sports and employment opportunities
  • Support for local business and better rural high-speed internet
  • Responsible budgeting and careful use of tax dollars
