OPTION 1 · VOTE ONLINE

Vote from wherever you are.

Online advance voting is available from Friday, October 9 at 9:00 a.m. until Saturday, October 24 at 11:59 p.m.

01

Confirm your voter information

Visit the Town’s voting portal and make sure you are on the voters list.

02

Opt in for online voting

Online voting requires an opt-in. The opt-in deadline is October 24 at 6:00 p.m.

03

Receive your PIN

Once approved by Elections staff, you will receive a confirmation email containing your unique voting PIN.

04

Cast your ballot

Use your PIN and date of birth to sign in and complete your online ballot before 11:59 p.m. on October 24.

LIBRARY SUPPORT

Halton Hills Public Library

Designated staff at the Georgetown and Acton branches can help residents check voter information, opt in, access a computer or internet connection, and navigate the voting website. Staff cannot assist with completing a ballot.
